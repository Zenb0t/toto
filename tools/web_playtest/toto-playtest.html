Toto

Start out on the open plains, find the mausoleum and go down. Monsters sleep or wander until they notice you; your blows and their shouts wake the rest. Gear is rare: search the floor, drink potions to survive, and take the stairs.

Left thumb
Move. The stick appears wherever you touch.
Attack
Tap to auto-aim, or drag to aim and release to fire.
Dash / Blink
Same gesture. Blink lands where you drag, over water but not through walls.
Skill
Shield, Frost Nova or Beckoning, once you find one. Own cooldown.
Potions
Tap one under the weapon switch to drink it.
Computer
WASD moves, the mouse aims, click (or hold) attacks, Space dashes or blinks, right click uses the skill, 1–3 drink potions.

Downloads about 10 MB the first time.
